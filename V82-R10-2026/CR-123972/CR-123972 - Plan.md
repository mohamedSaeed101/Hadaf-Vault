## Settled decisions (2026-10-04)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Partial save across the 7 tabs | **Per row + cascade.** Each row in each tab is judged on its own. A row in another tab whose item is rejected in the items tab is rejected too ("الصنف مرفوض في تبويب الأصناف"). Rows for items that already exist in the system are not cascaded |
| Base branch | Master_Stable → now `cr123972` (on origin, cut from a newer Master_Stable) |
| Barcode lookup (part 2) | **No AI** (changed 2026-10-05). `CatalogBarcodeProductSuggester` behind `IBarcodeProductSuggester`: Open Food Facts first (`product_type=all` also covers Open Beauty / Pet Food / Products Facts), then the free UPCitemdb trial API (~100 lookups/day per IP, no key). The OpenAI and Gemini providers were removed. |
| Arabic wording | Formal Arabic like the rest of the app, not the mockup's colloquial text |

## Part 1 — design
